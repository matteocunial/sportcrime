01 THE PREMISE

THE FIRST-EVER SPORTS INVESTIGATIVE SERIES

Former anchorwoman Daniela Goblin and rugby player & rock music soul Luka “Dabs” Kriv found SEAMS — a sports agency that quickly gets entangled in cases far beyond the surface of the game.

Dabs’s unique perception and sensitivity to sleuthing is crucial, and often pushes him to the edge. While the other “seamers” start to gel, Dabs has to deal with quirky, scary and conflictual relationships inside and outside the Agency. The death of an old teammate triggers a journey into the past.
